# Privacy

Blockiny runs locally in your browser.

## What it stores

The extension stores its settings in Chrome local storage:
- blocked sites
- current session data
- weekly schedules
- allowed YouTube pages
- local stats such as focused minutes, sessions, and blocked attempts
- notification and sound settings

## What it does not do

- It does not create an account.
- It does not send your data to a server.
- It does not use analytics.
- It does not sell or share personal data.

## Permissions

- `storage`: saves your settings and session data locally.
- `alarms`: switches between focus and break phases on time.
- `declarativeNetRequest`: redirects blocked pages to the Blockiny screen.
- `history`: suggests domains from your recent browsing history when you type a site.
- `notifications`: shows optional desktop notifications for phase changes and completed sessions.
- `offscreen`: plays optional focus sounds during focus rounds.
- `<all_urls>` host access: lets the blocker work on the sites you choose.

## Data location

All data stays on your device inside the browser profile that runs the extension.
